## Muaz Furkan Kılıç

I build AI systems for the energy sector. İstanbul.

Most of my work is forecasting and document understanding.

**Forecasting** — ensemble models predicting generation for wind and solar plants, where
being confidently wrong costs real money and the weather has no respect for your
validation split.

**Document understanding** — LLM-based extraction pipelines that read unstructured
paperwork and pull out the fields a person would otherwise retype by hand.

**The rest** is plumbing of the useful kind: a pricing engine, automatic classification
and routing for incoming requests, and agent systems built with LangGraph and n8n.

I also hold a Product Owner role on one of our teams, which has mostly taught me how much
of engineering is deciding what not to build.

About two and a half years in, heading deliberately toward AI and agentic engineering.
